Laundry renovations, south-east Melbourne

A good laundry is a room you do not have to think about. Machines at the right height, storage to the ceiling and a bench that works.

What is included

  • Stacked or side-by-side machine housings
  • Full-height storage and linen cupboards
  • New sinks, tapware and plumbing
  • Benchtops and splashback tiling
  • Hanging rails and pull-out baskets
  • Moving the laundry to a better spot

Designed around the machines

We build the joinery around the exact washer and dryer you own or are buying, with the clearances and ventilation they need.

Storage that earns its space

Every cupboard is planned for what goes in it, from brooms and the ironing board to baskets under the bench.

Finished like the rest of the house

Shaker doors in sage, oak and black, terracotta tile. A laundry deserves the same finish as the kitchen next door.

Can you move my laundry?

Often, yes. It depends on plumbing and drainage access, which we check on site before quoting.

Can the dryer be stacked on the washer?

Yes, with the right stacking kit or a joinery shelf, and ventilation planned for the dryer.

Do you renovate the laundry with other rooms?

Yes. Many clients do the laundry with the kitchen or bathroom to share the trades and the disruption.
