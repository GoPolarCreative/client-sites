How a project runs

At the first meeting, we listen. What works about the house, what does not, how you live in it and what you want to spend. Then we tell you honestly what is possible.

Before anything is pulled out, you have a fixed price, a program with dates and every selection agreed. No surprises in week three.

At handover, the house is clean, every trade's work is checked and you walk through it with the builder who built it.

What does Bhavan Building do?

We are a registered residential builder doing kitchen, bathroom and laundry renovations, full house renovations, extensions and knock-down rebuilds across Bentleigh East and south-east Melbourne.

Is Bhavan Building a registered builder?

Yes. We are a registered building practitioner in Victoria, registration CDB-U 102135.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. We visit, talk through what you want and give you a clear price and program.

How many projects do you take on?

A select number at a time, so every client deals directly with the builder and gets proper attention from start to finish.

Which suburbs do you work in?

Bentleigh East and the suburbs around it, from Brighton and Sandringham to Glen Waverley, Malvern, Camberwell and Kew.

Do you work with architects and designers?

Yes. We build from your architect's plans or bring in the designers, engineers and suppliers we trust.
