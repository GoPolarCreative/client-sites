Bathroom renovations, south-east Melbourne

Bathrooms, ensuites and powder rooms built from the waterproofing up. The part you never see is the part that decides whether it lasts.

What is included

  • Full strip-out and removal
  • Layout changes, walk-in showers and niches
  • Waterproofing to the Australian Standard
  • Floor-to-ceiling tiling
  • Vanities, tapware and fixtures
  • Lighting, heat and ventilation

Built from the waterproofing up

Falls to the waste, membranes and screeds are done before a tile goes on. It is the least glamorous part of the job and the one we are most careful with.

Details that hold up

Mixer heights, niche positions and tile set-out are agreed before the wall is closed, so the finished room lines up the way the drawing does.

Small rooms too

Powder rooms and ensuites get the same care as a main bathroom. Often the smallest room is where a finish shows most.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A typical bathroom runs a few weeks from strip-out to handover. We confirm your program once the design and fixtures are chosen.

Can you move the shower or toilet?

Usually, yes. Moving fixtures depends on where the drainage runs, which we check before quoting.

Is waterproofing included?

Yes. Waterproofing is part of every wet area we build, done to the Australian Standard before tiling.
