About Bhavan Building a select number of homes

A registered residential builder in Bentleigh East doing renovations, extensions and custom homes across Melbourne, with the builder on every job.

Why the name

Bhavan is the Hindi and Sanskrit word for a house. It is a fair summary of the job: homes that are beautiful, practical and built to stand the test of time.

Fewer projects, done properly

We do not cut corners or take on more work than we can manage. Focusing on a select number of projects means personal service, direct communication with the builder and real attention to detail throughout.

The people around the build

We work with trusted tradespeople, architects, engineers and suppliers to meet the highest standards of craftsmanship, compliance and finish, whether it is an extension, a full renovation or a custom home.
