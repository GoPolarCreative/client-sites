On a renovation we handle

  • Strip-out, capping and relocating services
  • Rough-in for in-wall mixers and wall-hung vanities
  • Freestanding baths and floor wastes
  • Laundry moves and bathroom-laundry combos
  • Kitchen sinks, dishwashers and fridge taps
  • Fit-off of tapware, toilets and basins

Planned with your other trades

A renovation stalls when one trade is late. We work to the program, tell your builder or tiler where we are at, and come back for fit-off when the room is ready, not a week later.

Set out right the first time

Mixer heights, waste positions and vanity set-outs are checked against the fixtures you have chosen before the wall is closed. That is what makes the finished room look as good as the photos.

Homeowners renovating direct

Renovation plumbing questions

Something else? Call Dan on 0423 017 489.

Can you move my toilet, shower or laundry?

Usually, yes. Moving fixtures depends on where the drainage runs, so we check on site and quote the move before anything is pulled up.

Do you supply the tapware?

Most clients choose their own fixtures. We confirm the rough-in parts suit what you have picked so the fit-off goes smoothly.

How do you work with my tiler and waterproofer?

We do rough-in before they start and fit-off after they finish, and we keep in touch so nobody is waiting on us.
