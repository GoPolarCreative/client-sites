Questions people ask

Something else? Call Dan on 0423 017 489.

Do you do emergency plumbing?

Yes. We take emergency and same-day jobs across Melbourne. Call 0423 017 489 and tell us what is happening. If water is running, turn it off at the mains first.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free. For renovations and new builds we quote from plans or a site visit, and the price is clear before work starts.

Do you work with builders and developers?

Yes. A lot of our work is alongside builders and developers on new builds and renovations, from drainage and rough-in through to fit-off.

Which areas do you cover?

All of metropolitan Melbourne, from the inner suburbs out to the north, west, east and south east. If you are not sure, call and ask.

Do you do commercial plumbing?

Yes. We do commercial work as well as residential, including repairs and maintenance for businesses and property managers.

What if the job changes once you start?

We tell you before we do anything extra and agree the price first. No surprise lines on the invoice.
