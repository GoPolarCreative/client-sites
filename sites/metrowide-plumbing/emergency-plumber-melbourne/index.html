Emergency plumber in Melbourne

Water where it should not be is not a job for next week. We take emergency and same-day callouts across Melbourne, and we tell you straight when we can be there.

Call 0423 017 489Free quote

Call us now for

  • Burst or split pipes
  • Water coming through a ceiling or wall
  • Blocked toilets and drains backing up
  • A leaking or failed hot water unit
  • Taps, cisterns or flexi hoses that will not stop running
  • No water at all

Before we get there

Turn the water off at the mains. The tap is usually beside the meter near the front boundary. If the hot water unit is leaking, switch it off at the isolation valve and the power or gas. Move anything valuable out of the way and take a few photos if you think you will claim on insurance.

What happens on the call

Tell us what you can see and which suburb you are in. We give you an honest time we can be there, and if it is something you can safely hold off until the morning, we say so.

Fixed properly, not patched

Once the water is under control we find the cause. If it needs a bigger repair we explain the options and the price before we do it.
